# Russian-language news coverage of Elering AS and CEO Kalle Kilk (2024–Sept 2026)

**Verification note on the time window:** The premise "Kilk became CEO ~April 2024" is **incorrect**. Per rus.err.ee (25 April 2023), Taavi Veskimägi announced he would step down on **1 July 2023** after ~14 years, and the supervisory board named management-board member **Kalle Kilk** as the new chairman of the board (CEO) from July 2023; Erki Säpp took Kilk's vacated board seat "until the end of 2024" — [ERR](https://rus.err.ee/1608958678/v-ijule-taavi-veskimjagi-pokinet-dolzhnost-predsedatelja-pravlenija-elering). The catalog below therefore covers mainly mid-2024 → Sept 2026 coverage, with the 2023 appointment article included as the succession announcement.

**Date caveat:** Dates marked "~" are approximate — inferred from article content, event dates, or rus.err.ee article-ID ordering (ERR URLs carry no date). Dates without "~" were confirmed by fetching the page. All URLs below were actually seen in search results or fetched; none are invented.

## Q1. Coverage of the BRELL disconnection / desynchronization from the Russian grid and synchronization with continental Europe (Feb 2025), and Kilk's statements

### Takeaway
Baltic Russian-language outlets (ERR, Postimees, LSM, Novaya Gazeta Baltия, Деловые ведомости) covered the 8–9 Feb 2025 BRELL exit intensively and largely positively, with Kilk personally front-and-center reassuring consumers that tariffs would not jump; one-year-later retrospectives concluded fears of price spikes did not materialize.

### Takeaway
Russian-language Baltic media covered the Eagle S incident and its legal aftermath closely; Kilk personally explained why Elering/Fingrid dropped the vessel arrest and went to court instead (litigation expected to take 5–6 years, repair up to ~€60M covered by the operators in the interim).
