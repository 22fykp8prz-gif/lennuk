# Estonian-language press coverage of Elering AS and CEO Kalle Kilk (2023–Sept 2026)

**Important date correction (verified):** Kalle Kilk did NOT become CEO in April 2024. His appointment as Elering's new juhatuse esimees was announced in late April 2023 (Postimees interview published 25 April 2023), and he formally took over the CEO position in **July 2023**, succeeding Taavi Veskimägi. In October 2024 the supervisory board (nõukogu, chaired by Gunnar Okk since 9 Feb 2024) re-elected/confirmed Kilk for a new five-year term (per Elering's English release, until 31 Dec 2029). The catalog below therefore runs from the April 2023 appointment announcement through September 2026.

Note on dates: dates marked "approx." are inferred from article context/IDs and adjacent events; dates without that marker were confirmed by fetching the page or by explicit dates in search results. All URLs below were actually seen in search results or fetched; none are invented.

## Kalle Kilk's appointment, re-election, and Elering leadership (juhatus/nõukogu)

### Takeaway
Kilk's appointment was announced April 2023 (took office July 2023, succeeding Taavi Veskimägi); a public competition for the chairman post was run in 2024 and on 11 Oct 2024 the nõukogu confirmed Kilk would continue for five more years. No salary scandals or leadership controversies surfaced in searches.

## Estlink 2 damage (25 Dec 2024, tanker Eagle S), repair, and undersea infrastructure

### Takeaway
Coverage ran from the 25 Dec 2024 outage (anchor damage by tanker Eagle S) through fault location (Jan 2025), repair progress, an ahead-of-schedule return in June 2025 (repair cost estimated at EUR 50–60m), and Elering/Fingrid's decision to sue the shipowner rather than pursue costly ship attachment; there was also a separate, earlier EstLink 2 fault in Jan 2024 covered during Kilk's first year.

## Electricity price, grid tariffs, reserves, generation capacity, investments, dividends

### Takeaway
Major threads: Elering's July 2025 application to raise the transmission tariff 37.6% (EUR 12.50→17.20/MWh, ~EUR 24.5m/yr, approved by Konkurentsiamet on 1 Feb 2026, effective 5 May 2026); the long-running frequency-reserves procurement (five winners, 236 MW, confirmed ~Oct 2025); two new balancing-cost lines on electricity bills from 2026; EstLink 3 planning (possible ~2035, >EUR 1bn); and state dividends (~EUR 15.7m paid in 2025 for 2024).
