# Elering AS under CEO Kalle Kilk — corporate, leadership and official-communications dossier (through Sept 2026)

**Important correction to the task premise:** Kalle Kilk did NOT start as CEO in April 2024. He became chairman of the management board on **1 July 2023** (initially until the end of the board's term, Dec 2024), succeeding Taavi Veskimägi, and was **re-elected after a public competition on 11 October 2024** with a five-year contract running to 31 December 2029.

## Key Question 1: When was Kalle Kilk appointed CEO, and what was his prior role?

### Takeaway
Kilk took over as chairman of the management board (CEO) on 1 July 2023 after Taavi Veskimägi resigned (announced ~Feb/March 2023, mandate ended 30 June 2023); Kilk had been an Elering management-board member since 13 February 2007 (essentially since the company's foundation), responsible for asset management (de facto CTO role). On 11 October 2024 the supervisory board re-elected him CEO for a term to 31 December 2029.

### Cited Findings
- Taavi Veskimägi announced his resignation after ~14 years as Elering CEO — [ERR News](https://news.err.ee/1608958723/elering-ceo-taavi-veskimagi-to-step-down-after-14-years); Elering's own release: [The CEO of Elering Taavi Veskimägi is resigning](https://elering.ee/en/article/ceo-elering-taavi-veskimagi-resigning); also [Baltic Times](https://www.baltictimes.com/the_ceo_of_elering_taavi_veskim_gi_is_resigning/)
- Elering press release dated 29 June 2023: from 1 July 2023 the board continued in renewed composition with **Kalle Kilk as chair (until Dec 2024)**, Riina Käi (CFO) and new member Erkki Sapp (former head of the energy markets department); Veskimägi's CEO mandate ended 30 June 2023 — [Elering, "The board of Elering will continue with a renewed composition from July 1"](https://elering.ee/en/article/board-elering-will-continue-renewed-composition-july-1)
- The same release states Kilk "has served as head of asset management and board member since Elering's foundation," and that Veskimägi, at the request of the Minister of Climate and Elering's supervisory board, continued advising the company on the Baltic synchronization project until synchronization took place — [Elering](https://elering.ee/en/article/board-elering-will-continue-renewed-composition-july-1)

## Key Question 4: Investment program headline numbers under Kilk

### Takeaway
The defining event was desynchronization from the Russian-controlled IPS/UPS grid and synchronization with Continental Europe on **9 February 2025** (total Baltic program ~€1.6bn, Estonia's share ~€298m, ~75% EU-funded). Elering invested €240.8m in 2024 and €181.0m in 2025; long-lead projects (EstLink 3 at 700 MW, a fourth ~1,000 MW Estonia–Latvia offshore link) slipped to completion around 2035–2040, and Elering ran major frequency-reserve procurements (rights to procure up to 8 years ahead, to 2033; up to 500 MW of new generation/storage; a €60m tender won by Enefit Power).

### Cited Findings
- Baltic TSOs sent formal notice (2024) to Russia/Belarus of decoupling from the IPS/UPS system in February 2025 — [Elering](https://elering.ee/en/article/baltic-tsos-have-sent-notice-decoupling-russia-controlled-electricity-system-february-2025)
- Synchronization with Continental Europe completed **9 February 2025**; total program cost **€1.6 billion** (large share for the Poland–Lithuania reinforcement), **Estonia's share €298 million**, EU covering ~75% — [Kliimaministeerium](https://kliimaministeerium.ee/uudised/oleme-valmis-baltimaade-sunkroniseerimiseks-euroopa-elektrivorguga); [European Commission Estonia representation, 9.2.2025](https://estonia.representation.ec.europa.eu/uudised/balti-riigid-uhinesid-mandri-euroopa-elektrivorguga-2025-02-09_et); regulator overview: [Konkurentsiamet yearbook 2024](https://aastaraamat.konkurentsiamet.ee/en/aastaraamat-2024/synchronisation-baltic-states-electricity-system-continental-europe)
- Kilk before synchronization: island-operation tests confirm the system can handle contingencies — [ERR, "Kilk: saartalitluse testid kinnitavad…"](https://www.err.ee/1609600238/kilk-saartalitluse-testid-kinnitavad-et-saame-ootamatustega-hakkama); Kilk's 18 Jan 2025 statement on synchronization — [Elering blog](https://elering.ee/artikkel/18012025-juhatuse-esimees-kalle-kilk-sunkroniseerimisest)
- Frequency reserves: Elering obtained the right to procure reserve capacity long-term (up to 8 years ahead, until 2033) and launched procurement of up to **500 MW** of new generation/storage capacity for independent frequency management; a tender deadline of 14 April 2025 was extended to **14 July 2025** — [Elering, "Elering launches procurement for long-term acquisition of new frequency reserves"](https://elering.ee/en/elering-launches-procurement-long-term-acquisition-new-frequency-reserves); cost-allocation model work: [Elering](https://elering.ee/en/article/elering-continues-work-approve-frequency-reserve-cost-allocation-model)
- Enefit Power won an Elering frequency-reserve tender worth ~€60 million — [CEEnergyNews](https://ceenergynews.com/electricity/enefit-power-elerings-60-million-euro-tender/) (date not verified in this pass)
- EstLink 3 (Estonia–Finland): planned 700 MW DC link; Elering applying for EU funding so construction cost would not hit consumer grid fees — [Elering EstLink 3 page](https://elering.ee/en/estlink-3); new Finland/Latvia links "at least 10 years" away — [ERR News](https://news.err.ee/1609880665/new-electricity-connections-between-estonia-and-finland-latvia-will-take-at-least-10-years)
- Fourth Estonia–Latvia interconnection: joint development with Latvia's AST, offshore route Saaremaa–Kurzeme, working assumption ~1,000 MW — [NS Energy](https://www.nsenergybusiness.com/news/elering-ast-fourth-estonia-latvia-interconnection/); [CEEnergyNews](https://ceenergynews.com/electricity/elering-and-ast-to-jointly-develop-fourth-estonian-latvian-electricity-interconnection/)
- Timeline slippage: fourth EE–LV link postponed to **at least 2038** — [ERR News](https://news.err.ee/1610023057/4th-estonian-latvian-electricity-connection-postponed-to-at-least-2038); Estonia plans new links to Finland and Latvia **by 2040**, investment decisions ~2031–2033 — [ERR News](https://news.err.ee/1610057188/estonia-plans-new-power-links-to-finland-and-latvia-by-2040)
- Grid "pre-development" (eelarendus) concept presented by Kilk (article dated 15 April 2024 per search result) — [Elering](https://elering.ee/artikkel/eleringi-juht-kalle-kilk-eleringi-elektrivorgu-eelarenduse-kontseptsioon-kasulik-tarbijale)
- Security of supply: Elering's assessment that supply security is ensured now and in the foreseeable future via dispatchable + renewable plants and interconnections — [Elering (Kilk statement)](https://elering.ee/artikkel/eleringi-juht-kalle-kilk-eleringi-hinnangul-varustuskindlus-praegu-ja-nahtavas-tulevikus); security-of-supply report headline "Varustuskindlus eeldab uusi juhitavaid elektrijaamu ja kriisikindluse kasvu" — [Elering](https://elering.ee/artikkel/varustuskindlus-eeldab-uusi-juhitavaid-elektrijaamu-ja-kriisikindluse-kasvu); until 2026 the state expects Eesti Energia to maintain 600–1,000 MW of capacity, then a strategic-reserve system (per search summary of the above sources — verify exact wording)
- Third Estonia–Latvia interconnection construction project (context, pre-window) — [Elering](https://elering.ee/en/article/elering-launched-third-construction-project-estonia-latvia-electricity-interconnection)

### Inferences
- Under Kilk the investment story pivots from the synchronization build-out (peaking 2024, completed Feb 2025) to grid renewal, frequency reserves/storage, and the next-decade interconnector pipeline — matching the falling capex (240.8 → 181.0 m€) and rising tariff needs.

## Key Question 5: Financial facts — 2024/2025 results, tariffs, dividends, congestion income

### Takeaway
Per Elering's investor page: 2024 revenue €214.3m, EBITDA €70.8m, net profit €15.7m, capex €240.8m, congestion income €80.8m, dividends paid €20.0m; 2025 revenue €300.5m, EBITDA €80.5m, net profit €21.7m, capex €181.0m, congestion income €6.7m, dividends paid €15.7m. In July 2025 Elering applied to raise the electricity transmission tariff 37.6% (€12.50 → €17.20/MWh) from 2026 — its first change since 2013 — and from 2026 two new balancing-related fees appear on bills.

### Cited Findings
- FY figures (as summarized from Elering's investor page): **2024** — revenue €214.3m, EBITDA €70.8m, net profit €15.7m, investments €240.8m (€83.0m EU co-financing), congestion income €80.8m, dividends paid €20.0m; **2025** — revenue €300.5m, EBITDA €80.5m, net profit €21.7m, investments €181.0m (€27.8m EU), congestion income €6.7m, dividends paid €15.7m; H1 2025 revenue €160.2m — [Elering, For investors](https://elering.ee/en/investors) *(extracted via automated summary; recommend re-verifying each figure against the page/annual reports before publication — note the €15.7m figure appears as both 2024 net profit and 2025 dividend, plausible under Estonia's practice of paying out prior-year profit but worth double-checking)*
- Moody's rating **A2/stable** for long-term debt (affirmation reported Feb 2024 on the investor page) — [Elering, For investors](https://elering.ee/en/investors)
- Annual report 2024: landing page — [elering.ee/en/annual-report-2024](https://elering.ee/en/annual-report-2024); PDF — [Elering_Annual-report_2024_ENG_03.pdf](https://elering.ee/sites/default/files/public/Investorile/MA%202024/Elering_Annual-report_2024_ENG_03.pdf)
- Tariff application (July 2025): Elering applied to the Competition Authority to raise the transmission tariff **37.6%, from €12.50 to €17.20/MWh, from 2026** — [ERR](https://www.err.ee/1609740600/elering-tahab-tosta-elektri-vorgutasu-37-6-protsenti); Elering release: [Elering taotleb elektri võrgutasu tõusu](https://elering.ee/en/article/elering-applies-increase-electricity-transmission-tariffs); also [Ehitusuudised, 8.7.2025](https://www.ehitusuudised.ee/uudised/2025/07/08/elering-tahab-tosta-elektri-vorgutasu-uuel-aastal), [Elektriala](https://elektriala.ee/elering-taotleb-elektri-vorgutasu-tousu/)
- Kilk's stated justification: tariff unchanged since 2013 while CPI rose ~56% and construction prices ~46% over ten years; Nord Pool congestion income had been used to absorb energy-crisis costs but can no longer offset them; line/substation renovation up to 3x more expensive than a few years ago, maintenance ~+40%; plus crisis-resilience investments (critical equipment reserves, emergency restoration systems) — [ERR](https://www.err.ee/1609740600/elering-tahab-tosta-elektri-vorgutasu-37-6-protsenti) and [Elering](https://elering.ee/en/article/elering-applies-increase-electricity-transmission-tariffs)
- From 2026, balancing costs split between producers and consumers; **two new fees** added to electricity bills — [Tööstusuudised, 19.09.2025](https://www.toostusuudised.ee/uudised/2025/09/19/elering-jagab-tasakaalustuskulud-tootjate-ja-tarbijate-vahel-2026-lisandub-elektriarvetele-kaks-uut-tasu)

### Inferences
- The collapse of congestion income (€80.8m in 2024 → €6.7m in 2025) after synchronization/market changes is the structural driver behind the 2026 tariff hike — this is the central finance narrative under Kilk.

## Key Question 6: Gas side — Balticconnector and gas market

### Takeaway
The Balticconnector Estonia–Finland gas pipeline, damaged by external force on 8 October 2023, was repaired in ~7 months: offshore repair completed with a successful pressure test on 4 April 2024 and commercial operation resumed on schedule on 22 April 2024 — the main gas-side milestone of Kilk's early tenure.

### Cited Findings
- Repair work at sea planned to start early March 2024 — [Elering](https://elering.ee/en/balticconnectors-repair-work-expected-start-sea-beginning-march)
- Repair completed; pipe integrity confirmed by water pressure test (4 April 2024); commissioning planned for 22 April — [Elering](https://elering.ee/en/balticconnectors-repair-work-completed-next-commissioning); Finnish counterpart: [Gasgrid, 4.4.2024](https://gasgrid.fi/en/2024/04/04/balticconnectors-repair-work-completed-next-on-to-commissioning/)
- Commercial use resumed as planned on **22 April 2024**; pipeline had been shut since the night of 8 October 2023 after external damage in Finland's EEZ; repair took ~7 months versus a typical 1–2 years — [Elering, "Repairs on Balticconnector have been successfully completed"](https://elering.ee/en/article/repairs-balticconnector-have-been-successfully-completed); [Offshore Energy](https://www.offshore-energy.biz/balticconnector-gas-pipeline-back-in-operation-following-six-month-shut-down/); [PGJ Online](https://pgjonline.com/news/2024/april/balticconnector-gas-pipeline-resumes-operations-following-damage)

### Inferences
- None beyond the above.
