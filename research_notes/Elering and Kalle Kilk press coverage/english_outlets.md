# English-language press coverage of Elering AS and CEO Kalle Kilk (July 2023 – September 2026)

## Verified facts: When did Kalle Kilk become CEO, and who is on the management board?

### Takeaway
Kalle Kilk became Chairman of the Management Board and CEO of Elering on **July 1, 2023** (not April 2024 as the assignment assumed), succeeding Taavi Veskimägi, whose departure was announced April 25, 2023. In October 2024 the supervisory board re-elected Kilk for five years, through December 31, 2029; the management board comprises Kalle Kilk, Riina Käi (CFO), Erkki Sapp (energy system management and markets) and Reigo Kebja (asset management).

### Cited Findings
- Taavi Veskimägi announced (article dated April 25, 2023) he would step down after nearly 14 years, effective July 1, 2023; Kalle Kilk appointed new board chair, Erkki Sapp named a new board member. Veskimägi quote: "For almost 14 years, I have had the privilege of working in a company, whose mission in the society is so clear - to keep the lights on and the rooms warm in Estonian homes." — [ERR News, "Elering CEO Taavi Veskimägi to step down after 14 years"](https://news.err.ee/1608958723/elering-ceo-taavi-veskimagi-to-step-down-after-14-years)
- Elering press release (June 29, 2023): from July 1, 2023 the management board consists of Kalle Kilk (chair; previously Elering's head of asset management and a board member since Elering's foundation), Riina Käi (CFO) and Erkki Sapp (formerly head of the energy markets department); Veskimägi's mandate ended June 30, 2023, and he continued advising on the Baltic synchronization project. — [Elering, "The board of Elering will continue with a renewed composition from July 1"](https://elering.ee/en/article/board-elering-will-continue-renewed-composition-july-1)
- Elering press release (October 11, 2024): supervisory board re-elected Kilk as Chairman/CEO, contract extended five years through December 31, 2029; other board members: Erkki Sapp and Riina Käi (contracts through December 31, 2026) and Reigo Kebja (asset management, contract through February 4, 2029). Supervisory board chairman Gunnar Okk: "Kalle's previous achievements and experience showcase his ability to address complex issues and effectively lead the Elering team." Candidate evaluation was done by Fontes PMP; the decision was by consensus. — [Elering, "The Supervisory Board of Elering re-elected Kalle Kilk as the Chairman of the Board"](https://elering.ee/en/article/supervisory-board-elering-re-elected-kalle-kilk-chairman-board)
- The re-appointment was also covered by trade press. — [CEENERGYNEWS, "Elering reappoints Kalle Kilk as Chairman and CEO"](https://ceenergynews.com/whos-in-charge/elering-reappoints-kalle-kilk-as-chairman-and-ceo/) (page blocked by bot verification on fetch; existence confirmed via search results, content not independently read)

### Inferences
- Because Kilk took over in July 2023, "coverage since his appointment" properly spans July 2023 onward; the catalog below therefore includes the October 2023 Balticconnector damage aftermath and April 2024 reopening, which fall inside his tenure.

### Gaps
- I found no English article reporting any management board departures/additions after the October 2024 re-election (e.g., whether Sapp's and Käi's contracts, expiring December 31, 2026, have been extended). No English coverage of supervisory board composition changes was found beyond the mention of chairman Gunnar Okk.

## What did English outlets report about the BRELL desynchronization (February 2025) and Kilk's statements?

### Takeaway
Coverage was extensive and uniformly framed the February 8–9, 2025 switchover as historic and geopolitically charged: Lithuania, Latvia and Estonia disconnected from IPS/UPS at 09:09 on Feb 8, ran ~24 hours islanded, and synchronized with Continental Europe on Feb 9 with no consumer impact. Kilk was the most-quoted Elering voice, calling it "the hardest thing we've done this century" and stressing that staying connected to Russia was riskier than leaving.

### Cited Findings
- Disconnection at 09:09 on Feb 8, ~24h isolated-operation tests, synchronization with Continental Europe Feb 9; no consumer impact. — [LSM English](https://eng.lsm.lv/article/economy/economy/08.02.2025-baltic-states-disconnected-from-russian-controlled-grid.a586997/); [The Baltic Times](https://www.baltictimes.com/baltic_countries_to_disconnect_from_russian_power_grid_and_join_europe_this_weekend/)
- Kilk: "about 20 years of effort... the hardest thing we've done this century"; no security incidents observed in cyberspace or the physical world during the transition. — [ERR News, Feb 9, 2025](https://news.err.ee/1609600253/elering-ceo-desynchronization-hardest-thing-we-ve-done-this-century)
